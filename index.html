The Gallery: Selected Works and Case Studies

In the 3D gallery room, you can walk through an exhibition of my most significant commercial and personal projects. Here is a detailed breakdown of the work created by Shaun.

Quota-Flow - AI Video Quota Aggregation Platform

Quota-Flow is a one-stop scheduling and allocation platform for AI video generation free quotas. It aggregates free video-generation quotas from 9 providers — Doubao, Qwen, Yuanbao, Dola, Zhipu Qingyan, Zhipu and more — into a single schedulable, observable, and shareable pool. Built with a modern React frontend, it lets users view quota status across vendors, coordinate usage across the team, and keep every generation task observable in real time.

Narr-Light - AI-Driven Story Script Platform

Narr-Light is an AI-driven, full-lifecycle platform for murder-mystery script creation, covering the whole chain of "creation assistance — content production — distribution & monetization — user community". Its core value is using AI to solve the industry's four major pain points: high creation barriers, long production cycles, high art costs, and difficult logic validation, making premium script games accessible to far more creators.

Astra-Studio - Enterprise AI Workbench

Astra-Studio is an enterprise-grade AI workbench built on RAG and Multi-Agent architecture. It fuses a real-time web search engine with a local knowledge base and persistent memory, and exposes a customizable Skills & Tools toolkit, enabling fully automated creative workflows from knowledge retrieval to file generation.
